                    Only the admin can change the gate.
                    
    
                    
    
                    
      
                    How it behaves
                    
      
                      
        
                    • Strict (default): nothing reaches the CTF without the form. A browser gets the form; curl, scripts and crawlers get a refusal that names the required fields. Tools must send the browser's cookie. Switched off, only browser page loads are asked and tools pass.
                      • 
        
                    • Queue (default): after the form the student sees a waiting page and gets in the instant you approve them in the Queue tab. Blocking from the queue also blocks them at the proxy.
                      • 
        
                    • The username goes into a signed cookie (30 days). Requests from the same IP without the cookie are matched to it, unless two students share that IP.
                      • 
        
                    • "Ask to rename" on a student rejects their name: their browser shows this form again and their other requests are refused until they answer.
                      • 
        
                    • Switching the gate off lets everyone straight through; students are then identified by the app's login or their IP.
